Six sphere-on-cube pairs — each pair shares a SceneMaterial with a different hatchPattern from the ANSI/ISO preset library: solid, ANSI32 steel (cross), ANSI36 masonry (brick lattice), ANSI37 insulation (wavy lines), ANSI38 dots, and a custom three-family fill. Brick / wavy / dots use dedicated SDF paths added beyond the original parallel-line family model. The spheres show how the hatch behaves on curved surfaces — particularly worth comparing across the three coordinate-space modes below.
Toggle the hatch space to compare modes:
  • screen: units in pixels, camera-locked. Zoom the canvas — lines stay at constant screen size.
  • world: units in world coords, locked to the world XY plane. Faces parallel to the hatch normal show no ink for that family (the planes never cross the face — try the box's vertical faces).
  • tangent: units in world coords, projected onto a per-fragment surface basis from dFdx/dFdy(vWorldPos). Every face shows the hatch regardless of orientation, like printed material.
