A scene of sphere-on-cube pairs sliced by two section planes. The "Caps" toggle flips view.effects.sectionPlaneCaps on and off — the cap shading itself is a stencil-based pass in the renderer that paints each plane's capColor only where the plane physically crosses the model. With caps off, every clipped fragment discards (clean cuts); with caps on, the cross-sections fill cleanly even for closed solids sitting entirely beyond a plane. The boxes carry a world-space orthogonal crosshatch and the spheres a world-space ANSI31 hatch.
The "Centre sphere" button flips ViewObject.clippable on the middle sphere — when off, that object ignores every active section plane and stays intact while everything around it gets sliced.
